Privacy

Deep Focus is built so your writing stays on your Mac.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Deep Focus is a Mac menu bar app run by Fieldwork (“we”). This page says what we collect, in the app and on this website, and what we don’t.

On your Mac

Your journal and stats live on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/Deep Focus. The journal is plain Markdown. Your app settings stay on your Mac too.

Subscriptions and payments

Payments are processed by Stripe: checkout and the billing portal are Stripe’s pages. We never see or store your full card number.

To check your license, our billing server stores an anonymous ID for your installation of Deep Focus, together with your Stripe customer and subscription IDs.

Email list

If you leave your email address on this site, it is stored by our email provider, Kit (ConvertKit). You confirm your address by a link before you’re added. We use it only for news about Deep Focus, and every email has an unsubscribe link.

This website

We use PostHog analytics to count visits to this site and a few actions on it: which pages are viewed, when a download, demo or signup button is clicked, when someone joins the email list, and when feedback is sent. We record that a signup or feedback message happened, not your email address or what you wrote. PostHog also receives the usual details a browser sends with any request, such as the page address, the site you came from and your browser type.

Our analytics sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device, so each visit is counted on its own and we don’t follow you across visits or other sites. We don’t record your screen or what you type. If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, nothing is recorded. PostHog processes this data for us in the United States.

Fonts load from Google Fonts. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs.

Feedback and email

The feedback form sends what you type to our feedback service: your message and, only if you include them, your email address and your Deep Focus and macOS versions. It is filed in our project tracker on GitHub so we can follow up.

If you email us, we use what you send only to help you. Please leave out journal contents, passwords and card details. We never need them.

Changes

If this page changes, we’ll post the new date at the top.
