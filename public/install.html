Deep Focus

Opening the early-access build

Apple hasn’t notarized this build yet, so macOS stops it the first time you open it. You only do this once.

  1. Open DeepFocus.dmg and drag Deep Focus into Applications.
  2. Open Deep Focus from Applications. macOS says it can’t check the app. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Scroll down and click Open Anyway next to the line about Deep Focus. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.
  4. Deep Focus opens and its icon shows up in your menu bar. When it asks for Accessibility access, turn it on so it can find the window you’re working in.

On macOS 14 (Sonoma) there’s a shortcut for step 3: Control-click Deep Focus in Applications, choose Open, then click Open again.
