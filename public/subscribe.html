Deep Focus

Open Deep Focus on your Mac to subscribe

Deep Focus is $9.99 a month. You subscribe from inside the app, so your subscription belongs to the Mac you use it on.

Choose Open Deep Focus, allow your browser to open the app, then choose Subscribe or manage billing in the app’s license window.

What happens next?

The app checks this Mac’s installation, then opens secure checkout or, for an existing subscription, the billing portal in your browser. Opening this page or the app does not subscribe you or change your subscription.

What if the app does not open?

On your Mac, open Deep Focus yourself and choose Trial and Subscription… from its menu bar menu. Then choose Subscribe or manage billing. If your browser asks for permission, allow it only when you mean to open Deep Focus.

What if Deep Focus is not installed?

You’ll need a Mac running macOS 14 or later. Download and open Deep Focus, then come back to this page.

What if I subscribed on another Mac?

Use the original installation to manage that subscription. A different or reinstalled copy may not recognize it. Don’t start another subscription to recover an existing one; contact support.

What if billing is unavailable in the app?

Opening the app does not start a subscription. If Subscribe or manage billing is missing or shows an error, contact support. Keep your existing installation and journal in place.
