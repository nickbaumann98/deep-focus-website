How do I leave focus mode?

Press and hold the “Hold to exit” button at the bottom of the screen until its ring fills, about five seconds. Letting go early resets it. If it stays stuck, quit Deep Focus from its menu bar menu.

What if a journal page is missing?

Keep the app and its data in place. Don’t delete, reset or replace your journal while we look into it.

How do I subscribe or manage billing?

Billing happens inside the app. See Subscribe or Manage subscription.

Should I send a crash report?

Start with the app version and what happened. If a crash report is needed, read it first and remove private paths, names, identifiers and document titles before you choose to share it.
