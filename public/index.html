What’s in it

The shortcut
⌘⇧F by default. Change it to anything you like.
Menu bar only
No Dock icon and no window to manage. It’s there when you press the keys.
A journal
Jot down what you’re about to work on, or how it went. Pages are plain Markdown files on your Mac, so they open in any editor, with or without the app.
Your focus time
When a session ends you get one line, like “You focused for 52 min, day 4 of your streak.” The journal keeps today, this week, your longest session and all time. It records only when and for how long, and keeps it on your Mac.

Questions

When does it launch?

Soon. Leave your email and we’ll write when it’s ready.

What Mac do I need?

Any Mac on macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later.

Why does it ask for Accessibility access?

macOS asks for it so Deep Focus can find the window you’re working in and put the walls around it.

Where do my journal and stats live?

On your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/Deep Focus. The journal is plain Markdown.
